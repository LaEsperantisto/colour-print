A note before you begin

This is an informal reflection tool inspired by the four-colour personality framework. It is not a scientifically validated psychological test. There are no right answers; choose what you would genuinely do, rather than what sounds best.

Your answers are processed in this page and are not sent to a server. Free-text explanations are used to help you reflect, but are not automatically interpreted by an AI.
